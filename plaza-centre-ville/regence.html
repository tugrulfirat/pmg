Designed for impact, the Régence Suite combines the vast scale of a convention center with the nuanced detailing of a luxury hotel. The space is defined by its massive, pillar-free floor plan, ensuring pristine sightlines for every attendee.

At the far end, a grand, stylish staircase provides a cinematic entrance for keynote speakers and wedding parties alike, while elegant crystal chandeliers and adjustable LED mood lighting allow for precise atmospheric control—from cool corporate focus to warm celebratory glows.
