Stay at the Intercontinental.
Use your points. Host here.

We are not a hotel. Your guests are never obligated to book a single room with us. Corporate clients affiliated with IHG, Marriott, or any other brand can accumulate their points at the hotel of their choice—and still hold their conference here. Maximum savings. Zero compromise.

No union. No surprise
line items. Ever.

Move a table? Fine. Want a different chair? Done. At a unionized hotel venue, every request becomes an invoice. Here, we roll up our sleeves—the owners are on-site—and what we say is included, is included. That's why clients who came once haven't left.
